FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Watch Party free?+

Yes — creating and joining rooms is completely free. A premium tier adds higher streaming quality, larger rooms and extra hosting controls.

Do my friends need an account?+

No. Anyone can join straight from the room link in their browser. An account only helps if you want to save rooms and friends for next time.

How many people can watch together?+

Up to 50 people can share a single synced room, all watching in step with live chat and reactions.

Which devices are supported?+

Any modern browser on laptop, Mac, Windows or phone — plus dedicated native apps for iOS and Android with picture-in-picture and background audio.
